# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in Awesome Web Agent Ecosystem.

We welcome contributions and participation regardless of experience level, background, identity, or perspective.

## Expected Behavior

All community members are expected to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcome different perspectives and experiences.
- Provide helpful, specific, and professional feedback.
- Respect project scope and contribution guidelines.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Help maintain a collaborative and welcoming environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insults, intimidation, or deliberately disruptive conduct.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Spam, malicious links, or abusive contributions.
- Unwelcome conduct that makes participation unsafe or hostile.
- Any other behavior that violates the principles of this policy.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may restrict or revoke participation when necessary to maintain a respectful community.

Enforcement decisions will be made with consideration for the nature and severity of the behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository maintainers privately through the contact or reporting options available on GitHub.

Please include relevant context and links where possible.
