# Submission Guidelines

Use these guidelines when proposing a new resource.

## Before submitting

- Confirm that the resource belongs to this repository.
- Check for an existing entry.
- Verify the official project URL.
- Read the project's documentation.
- Check its maintenance and compatibility information.
- Confirm that your description is accurate.

## Required information

A good submission contains:

1. Project name.
2. Official URL.
3. Appropriate category.
4. One-sentence factual description.
5. Relevant platform or compatibility details, when known.

## Formatting

Use:

`- [Project Name](https://example.com) — A concise factual description.`

## Quality rules

- Do not use referral or tracking links.
- Do not submit duplicate entries.
- Do not invent adoption, security, or compatibility claims.
- Do not present unofficial integrations as official.
- Do not add star counts or download statistics that quickly become outdated.
- Do not submit unrelated UI libraries or design resources.

## Security and responsible use

Resources that facilitate credential theft, unauthorized access, malware, or abusive scraping will not be accepted.

Web research and automation tools must be used responsibly and in accordance with applicable terms and laws.
